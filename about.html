MEET THE OWNER

Meet Ethan Bentley, owner of Bentley Outdoors.

Ethan Bentley, our owner, brings years of deep experience in horticulture, maintenance, and landscape design, driven by a genuine passion for the outdoors and plants. His love for nature fuels everything he does, from nurturing the smallest seedlings to crafting stunning outdoor spaces that thrive year-round.

In an industry often marked by poor communication and unreliable service, Ethan is committed to being the exception. He prioritizes honest, clear communication and delivers personalized solutions tailored to each client’s unique needs and property. His goal is not just to meet expectations but to build lasting relationships through exceptional customer care and an unwavering dedication to quality.
