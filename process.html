HOW WE WORK

From Concept to Creation.

Our four-step approach keeps your landscaping project clear—from the first conversation and outdoor plan to installation discussions and care guidance.

01

Consultation

Site Discovery & Brief

We begin by listening to how you wish to live or work outdoors, reviewing your site dimensions, property surroundings, sunlight patterns, and project priorities.

✓   Project Scope & Site Assessment Brief
02

Concept & Planning

Spatial Zoning & Direction

We translate your brief into clear spatial layouts—defining circulation paths, terrace zones, focal views, hardscape geometry, and initial botanical character.

✓   Conceptual Master Plan & Visual Direction
03

Design Development

Materials, Botany & Lighting

Every material joint, stone finish, drainage detail, lighting fixture, and climate-appropriate plant species is refined into an actionable specification.

✓   Detailed Planting, Hardscape & Lighting Plan
04

Implementation & Handover

Craftsmanship & Care Guidance

Our team coordinates civil hardscape works, soil conditioning, specimen planting, and night-lighting calibration—concluding with a clear seasonal care guide.

✓   Completed Landscape & Maintenance Guide
