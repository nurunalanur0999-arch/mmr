PRIVACY & WEBSITE FORMS

Your information, handled with care.

When you contact Bentley Outdoors, request an appointment or leave a review, the details you submit help us respond to your inquiry.

What we collect

Depending on the form, you may provide your name, contact details, project location, project notes, preferred appointment time, reference images or review text. We ask you not to include sensitive personal information.

How submissions are handled

This website uses Netlify Forms to receive requests. Form submissions are stored within the site's Netlify account and may be reviewed by Bentley Outdoors to respond to requests, discuss project work or review customer feedback.

Reviews

Submitted reviews are not automatically published. Only reviews selected for publication are added to the public website. An optional review email is for private follow-up, not public display.

Photos and external links

If you send reference images, they are used to understand your proposed project. Links to external sites, including Facebook or map services, follow those providers' own privacy practices.

Contact

For questions about information you sent through the website, email ebentley@bentoutdoors.com.
